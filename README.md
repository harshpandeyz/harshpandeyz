### 🛡️ Intelligent Mob Surveillance System

**See → Detect → Protect → Verify**

An AI-powered surveillance platform that turns suspicious visual events into secure and verifiable digital evidence.

**Built with**

`YOLOv8` `OpenCV` `FastAPI` `MongoDB`
`React` `Docker` `Jenkins` `Solidity` `Web3.py`

**System flow**

```text
CCTV
  ↓
AI Detection
  ↓
Suspicious Event
  ↓
Video Extraction
  ↓
AES-256 Encryption
  ↓
SHA-256 Hash
  ↓
Ethereum / Ganache
  ↓
Evidence Verification
```

The interesting part is not simply detecting an event. The system also protects the evidence, verifies its integrity, and gives the complete pipeline a deployable architecture.

### 🧬 QuantumMind AI

**Question → Retrieve → Reason → Respond**

A multimodal AI platform for quantum research that turns research documents and visual quantum circuits into searchable, contextual intelligence.

**Built with**

`React` `Spring Boot` `FastAPI`
`PostgreSQL` `FAISS` `RAG` `Docker`

**Core capabilities**

* Semantic search over research PDFs
* Retrieval-augmented generation
* Conversational AI with session history
* Streaming responses
* Quantum circuit image analysis
* JWT authentication and RBAC
* Docker-first deployment

The architecture deliberately separates the application layer from the AI service so the system can evolve independently.
